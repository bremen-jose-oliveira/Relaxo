# Privacy Policy — Relaxo

**Last updated:** 24 August 2026

## Controller

The controller for personal data relating to Relaxo is **Nexorumo**, a German sole proprietorship (Einzelunternehmen). Legally required identifying and contact details are set out in the Impressum at [https://nexorumo.com/impressum](https://nexorumo.com/impressum).

Relaxo is a baby care and sleep logging app operated by Nexorumo.

## Data we store on your device

Care logs (sleep, feeding, diapers, baths, wakes), baby profile details, chores, day tags, and app preferences are stored locally in SQLite on your phone.

## Cloud sync (optional)

If you sign in with Apple and join or create a household, selected care data is synced to our cloud provider (Supabase) so partners on the same household can share logs. Sync uses your account credentials and household membership.

Widget and Live Activity actions may use a short-lived access token stored in an App Group on your device to update shared sleep state when the app is not open.

## Third parties

- Apple (Sign in with Apple) — [apple.com/privacy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/)
- Supabase (optional cloud sync / auth) — [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)

## What we do not sell

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use care logs for advertising. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies in the app.

## Account deletion

You can delete your cloud account and wipe local data from Settings. Account deletion removes your household membership and deletes your authentication account. Shared household care data may remain available to other household members until they delete it.

## Children’s data

Relaxo is intended for parents and caregivers. Baby profile information is entered by you and treated as your household’s private care data.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, and port your data under applicable law (including the GDPR where it applies). To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in the Impressum or email nexorumo@protonmail.com.
